01 / 06 · Ravni krov i terasaFlat roof & terrace

Ravni krov koji prokišnjavaA leaking flat roof

Ravni krovovi i krovne terase na kućama iz 70-ih i 80-ih imaju hidroizolaciju staru 30 i više godina. Voda stoji, membrana puca uz atiku, a mrlja na stropu pojavi se tek kad je ploča već natopljena. Flat roofs and roof terraces on houses from the 70s and 80s carry waterproofing that is 30 or more years old. Water ponds, the membrane splits at the parapet, and the ceiling stain only appears once the slab is already soaked.

  • Lokve koje stoje danima nakon kišePuddles that sit for days after rain
  • Ispucala ili napuhnuta membrana uz ruboveCracked or blistered membrane along the edges
  • Mrlje i ljuštenje boje na stropu zadnjeg kataStains and peeling paint on the top-floor ceiling

02 / 06 · Kupaonica i instalacijeBathroom & plumbing

Puknuta cijev u ziduA burst pipe inside the wall

Pločice izgledaju netaknuto, ali cijev iza njih curi mjesecima. Račun za vodu raste, fuge tamne, a vlaga putuje kroz zid u susjednu prostoriju ili u stan ispod. The tiles look untouched, but the pipe behind them has been leaking for months. The water bill climbs, the grout darkens, and moisture travels through the wall into the next room or the flat below.

  • Neobjašnjivo visok račun za voduAn unexplained jump in the water bill
  • Tamne fuge i odignute pločiceDark grout lines and lifting tiles
  • Vodomjer se vrti kad je sve zatvorenoThe water meter turns with every tap closed

03 / 06 · Zidovi prizemljaGround-floor walls

Kapilarna vlagaRising damp

Starije kuće od kamena i opeke često nemaju horizontalnu hidroizolaciju. Vlaga se iz tla diže kroz zid, nosi soli koje razaraju žbuku i stvara plijesan iza namještaja. Older stone and brick houses often have no damp-proof course. Moisture rises from the ground through the wall, carrying salts that break down the plaster and growing mould behind the furniture.

  • Valovita linija vlage pri dnu zidaA wavy tide mark low on the wall
  • Bijele soli i žbuka koja se mrviWhite salts and crumbling plaster
  • Miris vlage i plijesan u kutovimaA musty smell and mould in the corners

04 / 06 · Podovi i pločaFloors & slab

Podovi koji nisu u ravniniFloors that aren't level

Parket koji se spušta prema zidu, vrata koja više ne zatvaraju i pukotine u pločicama znak su slijeganja, oštećene ploče ili lošeg estriha. To se ne popravlja novim podom. Parquet that slopes toward a wall, doors that no longer close and cracked tiles point to settlement, a damaged slab or failing screed. A new floor won't fix it.

  • Vrata se sama otvaraju ili zapinjuDoors swing open by themselves or stick
  • Razmak ispod lajsniA gap opening under the skirting
  • Pucanje pločica i odizanje parketaCracking tiles and lifting parquet

05 / 06 · Nosivi zidovi i fasadaLoad-bearing walls & facade

Konstrukcijske pukotineStructural cracks

Kosa pukotina koja kreće iz kuta prozora nije estetski problem. Nakon potresa u Zagrebu i Petrinji mnoge kuće imaju oštećenja koja su samo prebojana. Prije kupnje treba znati je li pukotina stara, aktivna ili opasna. A diagonal crack running from a window corner is not cosmetic. After the Zagreb and Petrinja earthquakes, many houses carry damage that has simply been painted over. Before you buy, you need to know whether a crack is old, active or dangerous.

  • Kose ili stepenaste pukotine od kutova otvoraDiagonal or stepped cracks from window and door corners
  • Pukotine šire od 2 mmCracks wider than 2 mm
  • Svježe prebojani dijelovi fasadeFreshly repainted patches on the facade
Sanacija ako se ne otkrije na vrijemeRepair if not caught in time €5.000 – 50.000 Zbroj do sadaRunning total€17.500 – 100.000

Mjerenje i dokumentiranje pukotina, procjena uzroka i preporuka statičara kad je potrebno.Crack measurement and documentation, an assessment of the cause, and a referral to a structural engineer when needed.

06 / 06 · Odvodnja ispod zemljeBelow-ground drainage

Zgnječene odvodne cijeviCrushed drain pipes

Oborinska odvodnja ispod zemlje pukne od korijenja, slijeganja ili teških vozila. Svaka kiša tada ide ravno u temelje: vlažan podrum, pukotine i slijeganje kuće. Underground stormwater drains crack from tree roots, settlement or heavy vehicles. Every rainfall then goes straight into the foundations: a damp basement, cracks and a settling house.

  • Voda se zadržava uz temelje nakon kišeWater pooling along the footings after rain
  • Vlažan podrum ili mrlje pri dnu fasadeA damp basement or stains low on the facade
  • Oluk koji se prelijeva ili odvod koji grgljaOverflowing gutters or gurgling drains

Nova gradnja nije jamstvo.A new build is no guarantee.

Novi stan ili kuća dolaze s garancijom izvođača, ali ona vrijedi samo ako nedostatke prijavite na vrijeme i s dokazima. Na novogradnjama redovito nalazimo ista curenja i pukotine kao na kućama starima 40 godina. A new apartment or house comes with the builder's warranty, but it only helps if you report defects in time and with evidence. On new builds we regularly find the same leaks and cracks as on 40-year-old houses.
