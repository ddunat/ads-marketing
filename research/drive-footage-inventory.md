## How to read this

- **Orientation and duration are not known for any clip.** The Drive connector returns name, size, dates and parent folder, but not `videoMediaMetadata` (width, height, duration). The Draft Desk pages (`Draft Desk` for HR and `Draft Desk AU`) record which Drive file each draft was cut from, so any file a draft used gets that draft's title and topic. Files no draft used are marked "unknown". Clips in the landing-page folder have descriptive names, so their topics come from the names.

- Duplicates: the inboxes hold many byte-identical re-uploads (same name and size). They are collapsed to one row, with the other IDs in the notes.
