# Angle Matrix (adopted 2026-11-05)

Source: an Instagram reel David sent on 2026-11-05 ("Why your new ads keep losing to your old winner", by a media buyer spending $6M a month). Our copy is in Arcads as `reference_reel_DfXrim6xnyH`. Its principle, applied to DC Building Inspectors.

## The principle
- **An angle has three parts:** one reason to buy, aimed at one kind of person, at one awareness stage.
  - A new hook, edit or image on the same pitch is a new *version*, not a new angle.
- **The angle is the targeting.** Meta reads the ad to decide who sees it.
  - The same angle keeps finding the same people.
  - A new angle opens a new pocket of the audience.
- **The grid:** 8 desires × 5 awareness stages = 40 angles per offer.
  - The 8 desires are the "Life-Force 8" from Drew Eric Whitman's *Cashvertising*: survival, food, safety, attraction, comfort, winning, family, approval.
  - The 5 stages are from Eugene Schwartz's *Breakthrough Advertising*. What each stage needs:

| Stage | What the ad must do |
|---|---|
| Unaware | Tell a story |
| Problem aware | Name the pain |
| Solution aware | Show why our way beats the others |
| Product aware | Proof |
| Most aware | Just the offer |

- **Testing:**
  - Put one angle in each ad set, with 3–5 versions inside it.
  - Let each angle spend 2–3× the target cost per lead before judging it.
  - Don't put 30 ads in one ad set: Meta only spends on 3–4 of them.
- **Expect most new angles to fail.** One winner pays for all of them.

### Our evidence
- The HR ad set holds 9 desk ads plus the pre-desk ads.
- In the last 7 days to 5 Nov, Meta put almost all spend on E3, A2, F1 and E1. B2, C2, D1 and G1 got €2–16 each.
- That matches the reel exactly: those four never got a fair test.

### Desire wording for us
- We don't use "food" or "attraction".
- "Survival" means structural safety, never health claims. There are no mould health claims (CLAUDE.md and the HR rules).
- No Meta personal-attribute framing anywhere. "Family" means *the people who'll live in the house*, never "are you a parent?".

## Testing structure
- **New ads:** each new angle gets its own ad set, holding 3 versions of that angle.
  - Versions are different hooks or openers on the same reason, person and stage.
  - Build ad sets and ads **paused** with the same targeting as the current test ad set. They only spend once David taps approve.
- **Live winners:**
  - HR A2/F1/E1 and AU A2 stay where they are.
  - Moving a live winner resets its learning, so we don't touch them unless David says to.
- **Starved ads:**
  - HR B2, C2, D1 and G1 never got a fair test in the crowded ad set.
  - Proposal on the HR desk: move each into its own angle ad set next time it's rebuilt. Don't count them as failed.
- **Kill thresholds already match "2–3× target KPI":**
  - HR: €60 with 0 leads = 3× €20.
  - AU: $60 with 0 leads = 2× $30; $90 = 3×.
