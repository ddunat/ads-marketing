## Competitors

## Angles to adapt (don't copy)
**Pre-purchase**
- No cooling-off at Victorian auctions, so inspect before you bid.
- Speed before the deadline.
- Hidden costs shown in dollars.
- The report as negotiation leverage.
- Tiered packages with a "most popular" anchor.
- Credentials.

**Construction**
- The leverage window.
- Last look behind the walls (pre-plaster).
- Inspect before paying the stage claim.
- Set expectations on defect counts.
- Re-inspection proof.
- Warranty evidence.
- "You're allowed on site" (DBC Act s19).
- The fair-inspector twist.

## White space for DC
1. Same-day report or you don't pay. This is the strongest guarantee in the market, but it needs precise written conditions.
2. Local Google leadership: 4.9 from 529 vs Owner Inspections' 4.7 from 428. Name the platform and the date.
3. Friendly, light humour. No competitor does it.
4. Sequenced stage-by-stage creative for volume-builder first-home buyers (good for retargeting).
5. Builder-friendly "second set of eyes" positioning. Show good builds too.
6. Price transparency, *or* hidden prices (to test; David leans toward hiding).
7. On-site walk-and-talk explanation.
8. Growth-corridor localisation: Tarneit, Wyndham, Clyde North, Casey, Melton, the north, Armstrong Creek.
9. Correct regulator naming. The VBA was replaced by the BPC on 1 Jul 2025.

## Compliance
- **Guarantee:** state the conditions (cut-off time, business days, access delays). Don't imply it limits consumer guarantees.
- **"Best" or "No.1":** avoid. Use verifiable facts with a date.
- **Statistics:** use only sourced figures. The CIE figure of about 1.04 defects per dwelling is usable. The "70% of homes" figure is unsourced.
- **Visual inspection limits:** never imply every defect will be found (AS 4349.1).
- **Prices:** show GST-inclusive totals.
- **"VBA registered" is outdated.** Use "BPC-registered (formerly VBA)" once confirmed.
- **Meta personal attributes policy:** no "you're a first-home buyer" or "can't afford…" framing.
- **Housing special ad category:** check in Ads Manager. Ads may be flagged.
- **Footage:** don't show builder brands or signage. Get client consent. Blur addresses and faces.
