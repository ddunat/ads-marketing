# Jeremy AI scoring rubric (captured 2026-10-12)

Source: Jeremy AI via the `jeremy` MCP server, conversation `809d18b5-6d1f-410a-b9f0-358647e4d345`. Pass mark: 8/10.

## Criteria and weights
| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Directness | ~30% | Says exactly who it's for, what they get and what to do next, in the fewest words. No cleverness for its own sake, no jargon the customer wouldn't say. Talk to the specific buyer (e.g. a homeowner with a contract deadline), not abstract "investors". |
| Hook placement | ~25% | The strongest line (the same-day-or-money-back guarantee) is in the **first 3 seconds** of every video. Buried at 0:15 = automatic deduction. |
| Congruency | ~20% | The landing page repeats the ad's promise **in the same words**. Ad says "report same day or your money back" → page headline must too. |
| Variance | part of 25% | Enough distinct hooks and headlines in a batch that we're actually testing something. |
| Social proof placement | part of 25% | 529 reviews at 4.9★ used as a **mid-ad** proof point, not a closing afterthought. |
| Accessibility | part of 25% | Captions on every video (most feed viewing is silent). |

## Score bands
- **6:** competent but generic. Names the service, lists benefits, has a CTA, but could belong to any Melbourne inspector. The hook appeals to a minority motive.
- **8 (pass):** direct, congruent, hook-first, real proof, a clear reason to act now.
- **10:** the hook reframes the buying decision itself. The proof is specific and undeniable, the urgency is real, and no line can be cut. Run it as written.

## Most common failures (in order)
1. Guarantee or best line buried mid-video instead of leading.
2. Written for the needs-convincing crowd instead of in-market buyers (inflates CPL).
3. Ad and landing page don't match.
4. Body copy as one wall of text instead of short, spaced lines.
5. No captions.
6. Claims that trip Meta review:
   - "Or your money back" is fine.
   - Never imply defects are guaranteed to be found.
   - Careful framing for health-risk language around pests and mould.

## Draft format to send Jeremy
1. Business and market.
2. Campaign objective and optimisation event.
3. The hook in one line.
4. Full script or copy, with timestamps for video.
5. The landing page headline the click lands on.
6. Every claim or number used.

Jeremy replies with a score out of 10 and line-by-line notes. Below 8, he rewrites the weak section himself.
